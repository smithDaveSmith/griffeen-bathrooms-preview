About this concept

Privacy
in this preview.

A plain explanation of how this unofficial website concept behaves.

Contact details stay in your browser.

The enquiry tool prepares a draft email locally. It has no submission endpoint and does not send your form entries to Griffeen or a separate form service. It does not write form data to cookies or browser storage.

When you choose “Open my email app”, the draft is handed to your configured email application. Sending it is a separate action you take in that application.

Images, fonts and motion files are local.

The concept self-hosts its photographs, fonts and GSAP. It adds no analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps or social widgets. External links and phone/email actions open the respective services only when selected.

Hosting is a separate service.

The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve and protect this preview. This notice describes the website code and is not a substitute for the hosting provider’s own privacy terms.

This is not the business’s legal policy.

The business owner must confirm the data controller, approved contact handling, retention, service providers, cookies and any analytics before a live launch. The original site’s cookie policy describes a different implementation.

Questions for the business.

Use info@griffeenbathrooms.ie or 01 504 0831 for business enquiries. This concept has no live booking, payment or account system.

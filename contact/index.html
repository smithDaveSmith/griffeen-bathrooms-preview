Before you get in touch.

Is a quote a commitment to proceed?

Griffeen’s published process says its quotation is not a contract and you may choose not to proceed after receiving it.

Can I book an installation date online?

No. The published process places the showroom appointment and agreed quotation before booking a date and paying a deposit. Contact the team to discuss current availability.

How much will my bathroom cost?

The source site does not publish fixed renovation prices. The team measures your bathroom and prepares an itemised quotation for your agreed scope and choices.

Can I visit on Saturday?

The published Saturday hours are 10am–1pm, by appointment. Call first to arrange your visit.

The new art direction.

The revision uses the authentic lavender Griffeen logo, a central mirror-shaped photographic opening and a GSAP drawing / detail / room scroll scene. On wide screens the scene holds one real project in view; mobile and reduced-motion views use direct comparison controls. Large photographic radii follow the mirror and basin geometry; practical form fields use a smaller 14px radius.

The material: Drawing.

The creative direction comes from a concrete part of Griffeen’s process: the showroom drawing used to walk through and customise the customer’s itemised quotation. The interactive Churchtown scene is a new illustrative tracing of the actual published photograph. It is not an original Griffeen drawing and contains no invented measurements.

Authentic images, limited claims.

All 75 project photographs were sourced from the business’s published Recent Projects page. Original watermarked source files have been retained; layout crops are used in selected opening panels. No generated room, team, showroom or customer imagery is used. Project names and places follow the source captions; “City Center” is normalised to “City Centre”. Publication dates, costs and installation durations are not inferred.

The short Brian G. quote is an attributed excerpt from the original website, retaining its wording. It is not an independently verified review or a star rating.

Owner confirmation before launch.

  • Approve image reuse, logo direction, copy and current project captions.
  • Confirm address, opening hours, service area and scope.
  • Resolve experience claims and supply evidence for any qualification claims.
  • Confirm lead times, deposit arrangements and written warranty terms.
  • Authorise and configure a real form endpoint and its privacy handling.
  • Approve live legal pages, redirects, domain and any analytics.

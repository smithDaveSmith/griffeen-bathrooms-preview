From the first idea
to the final detail.

A clear sequence from your enquiry to your finished bathroom. Based on Griffeen’s published renovation process.

The steps to
your new bathroom

  1. 01

    Your first enquiry

    Contact the team by phone, email or through the original website. Tell them what you have in mind and where your property is.

  2. 02

    A visit to your property

    Griffeen’s project manager visits by arrangement to look at the existing bathroom, take measurements and record your ideas.

  3. 03

    An itemised quotation

    The published process promises a breakdown showing how the quotation total is reached. Receiving a quotation is not a contract or an obligation to proceed.

  4. 04

    Choices in the showroom

    A showroom appointment uses a drawing to walk through the quotation and customise the project. Once you agree to proceed, the installation date is booked and a deposit is requested.

  5. 05

    Preparing for installation

    The source process says products are ordered two weeks before work begins. Confirm the current ordering schedule, availability and deposit amount with the team.

  6. 06

    Fitting and follow-up

    Work starts on the agreed date. The published process includes a follow-up one week after completion. It states a two-year labour warranty and manufacturer guarantees; ask for the current written terms and exclusions.

Your quote comes
before your commitment.

There is no published fixed renovation price on the source website. Your room, choices and agreed scope are used to prepare a detailed quotation.

Ask the team to confirm the current lead time, installation duration, deposit and written warranty terms for your project.
